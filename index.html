    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I start a project?

    Fill out the contact form below or message us on WhatsApp. We'll respond with a short brief, timeline, and quote — usually within a few hours during working days.

    How long does delivery take?

    Single creatives typically take 24–72 hours. Full brand identity systems take 5–10 days depending on revisions and scope.

    How many revisions are included?

    Starter includes 2 revisions, Business includes 5, and Premium includes unlimited revisions within the project scope.

    Do you provide source files?

    Yes. Business and Premium packages include editable source files (AI, PSD, Figma, or SVG depending on the project).

    How does payment work?

    We typically take 50% advance to begin and 50% on delivery. UPI, bank transfer, and international options are available.

    Do you work with clients outside Kerala/India?

    Yes. We work with clients globally over email, WhatsApp, and Zoom. Time zones are handled with async updates.
