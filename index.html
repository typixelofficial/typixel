Case Study

Chamayam Textiles

Brand Evolution · 2024

The Challenge

A legacy textile brand struggling to connect with a modern, younger audience.

The Approach

Merging traditional weaving motifs with contemporary, minimalist design principles.

The Solution

A complete brand refresh, cinematic social campaigns, and a simplified logo system.

The Result

A 40% increase in social engagement and a modernized brand presence that honored its heritage.
