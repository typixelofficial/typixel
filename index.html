    FAQ

    Common questions

    What graphic design services does Typixel offer?

    We offer comprehensive branding, logo design, social media creatives, web design, packaging design, print design, and YouTube thumbnail design for businesses of all sizes.

    Do you work with businesses outside Kerala?

    Yes. While we are based in Malappuram, Kerala, we work with clients globally over email, WhatsApp, and Zoom. Time zones are handled with async updates.

    How does the design process work?

    Our process follows four clear steps: Discovery (understanding your goals), Strategy (mapping direction), Design (iterative feedback), and Launch (final asset delivery).

    How long does logo design take?

    A single logo concept typically takes 24–72 hours. A full brand identity system takes 5–10 days depending on revisions and scope.

    How many revisions are included?

    Revisions are included within the agreed project scope. Starter includes 2 revisions, Business includes 5, and Premium includes unlimited revisions within the project scope.

    Do you provide source files?

    Yes. Business and Premium packages include editable source files (AI, PSD, Figma, or SVG depending on the project).

    How does payment work?

    We typically take 50% advance to begin the project and 50% on delivery. UPI, bank transfer, and international options are available.
